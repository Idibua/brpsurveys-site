Participant Information

Welcome to BRP Surveys

You are likely visiting this site because you received an email invitation from BRPSurveys.com to participate in a market research survey. BRP Surveys conducts market research on behalf of our clients, and your contact information was shared with us specifically for this purpose.

As part of their privacy and data management policies, our clients typically inform individuals that when you consent and opt-in to receive communications or provide your information, it may be shared with trusted partners to support activities like market research.

We do not conduct additional or ongoing follow-up communications beyond the survey process.

Your Privacy

We collect:

  • Information you provide, such as survey responses or inquiries through a survey platform provider/partner
  • Technical information (IP address, cookies)
  • Information shared by clients to enable survey outreach

We use this information solely to:

  • Conduct research
  • Understand market perspectives and trends
  • Fulfill requests of our clients
  • Comply with legal obligations
Important:
• We do not sell your personal information.
• We do not use third-party advertising cookies.
• We do not track your activity across unrelated websites.

Our Full Privacy Policy is available here: Privacy Policy

A Note About Incentives

Some studies include incentives for your participation. You would have been made aware of the incentive in your invitation to the market research effort. Please note:

  • You will need to complete a short set of screening questions before qualifying for the survey.
  • Based on your answers, you may be informed that the quota for your segment is already full.
  • Alternatively, you may be informed that you do not qualify for this particular survey.
  • If you do not qualify or the quota is full, you will not receive an incentive.
  • If you qualify and complete the survey, BRP Surveys will follow up within a few days of the close of the survey to provide your incentive (this may be up to a week or more following your completion of the survey).
